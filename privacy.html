Privacy Policy

ClearSlate Mac, published by Pooja Shah. Last updated 2 November 2026.

The short version: ClearSlate Mac does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it looks at stays on your Mac.

What the app looks at on your Mac

To find things you can clear, ClearSlate Mac reads the names, sizes and dates of files and folders in the places you allow: your home folder and, if you choose to remove apps, the Applications folder. It also reads which apps are installed, which are open, and your Mac's free space, memory, processor and battery figures. This happens on your Mac only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app keeps on your Mac

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter it, to greet you on the Home screen.
  • Your settings and the permissions you gave (the folders you allowed).
  • A list of what you cleaned and the cleaned items themselves, in a safety folder for 7 days so you can put them back.
  • A small history of storage sizes (numbers only) used to show what grew.

All of this lives in the app's own folders on your Mac. Deleting the app and its folders removes it.

Network

ClearSlate Mac makes no network requests of its own. Purchases go through Apple's App Store (see below).

Purchases

Scanning is free. If you buy cleaning (yearly or lifetime), the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email, payment card or Apple Account. The app only learns from the App Store whether this Apple Account has access. Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional monthly reminder, macOS shows one notification on the 1st of the month. It is created on your Mac and does not use a server.

Your choices

  • You can stop allowing a folder at any time in the app's Settings, or in System Settings under Privacy and Security.
  • You can put cleaned items back, or empty the safety folder, from Recently cleaned.
  • You can cancel a yearly plan in your Apple Account.

Children

ClearSlate Mac is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever collects any data, this page will be updated first and the new date shown above.
