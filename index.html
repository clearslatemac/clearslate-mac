ClearSlate Mac support

Free up space on your Mac safely. Nothing is deleted straight away: everything you clean waits 7 days in a safety folder.

Need help? Email clearslatemac@gmail.com. Tell us your macOS version and what you were trying to do, and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I get something back?

Open Recently cleaned in the sidebar. Press Put everything back for a whole clean, or open it and put back one item. Putting items back is always free.

Why does the app ask for a folder?

macOS keeps apps from reading your files unless you allow it. ClearSlate Mac asks once for your home folder so it can look for caches and leftovers, and once for the Applications folder the first time you uninstall an app, so it can move the app out and put it back later. You can stop allowing either folder in Settings. The app reads names and sizes only.

What is free and what is paid?

Scanning is always free. Cleaning needs a plan: yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Both are bought through the App Store.

How do I cancel or manage my yearly plan?

Open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, then Subscriptions. You can also press Manage… in ClearSlate Mac's Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought it before. How do I get it back on a new Mac?

Open Settings and press Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I remove ClearSlate Mac?

First put back anything you still want from Recently cleaned. Then quit the app and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
